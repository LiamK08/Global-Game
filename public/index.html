      How to play

      A secret mystery country is chosen at random.

      • Type any country and press Guess. The globe spins to it.
      • Each guess is coloured by distance to the target — pale when far, deep red when close.
      • The bar at the bottom shows how close your nearest guess is.
      • Find the country in as few guesses as possible. Then play again — it's unlimited!
